DICTATOR

Student Dictation Tool

DICTATOR is built to help students revise by ear instead of only reading by eye. It turns notes, study summaries, and essays into a listening session so revision feels calmer and more manageable.

Try the tool

Best for

  • Essay revision and course note review.
  • Vocabulary practice and language learning support.
  • Listening to important ideas in shorter, easier-to-follow groups.

How it helps

  1. Paste notes or upload documents directly from your device.
  2. Use smaller reading groups for easier comprehension.
  3. Listen and repeat until the idea is clear.

Frequently asked questions

What makes it useful for students?

It turns reading material into spoken audio so students can absorb it through repetition and listening.

Can it help with language learning?

Yes. Repetition and short audio groups can make vocabulary and reading practice easier to internalize.

Can I save progress?

Yes. Projects are saved in the browser so you can return to the same study session later.
